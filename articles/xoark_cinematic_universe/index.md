# Xoark Cinematic Universe

In a blog post from the 2026.08.10 I detailed the XoArK cinematic universe. I made bold claims such as: creatures indicate the style of the music, and that there's is a secret hash hidden in the titles of the 1-3 single releases. I wont be able to prove any of this yet, however I will try to create a somewhat comprehensive map of the album groups in XoArK's discography and my progress on them.
