Xoark Cinematic Universe

In a blog post from the 2026.08.10 I detailed the XoArK cinematic universe. I made bold claims such as: creatures indicate the style of the music, and that there's is a secret hash hidden in the titles of the 1-3 single releases. I wont be able to prove any of this yet, however I will try to create a somewhat comprehensive map of the album groups in XoArK's discography and my understanding of them.

P()~()..X (2021 - XXXX)

This series of albums acts as a great introduction to XoArK's main styles. The focus is mostly on breakcore or IDM, and the albums flow nicely due to the inclusion of ambient and noise interludes. While these works aren't particularly conceptual (excluding P()~()..16) they feature some of XoArK's choppiest breaks and most trance-inducing rhythms. A bit of XoArK's evolution as an artist is also encapsulated in each release. So, whichever sound you end up liking you're sure to find more by perusing their other works from the same time.

P()~()..32

P32 is possibly one of XoArK's most consistent albums, and a great intruduction to the style I associate with their notebook creature drawings. I'd describe it as breakcore infused with glitch and IDM elements. The track trelp3 has some of the choppiest breaks of any XoArK release I've heard to date.


P()~()..16

P16 is an anomaly. Other albums in the series primarily focus on snappy drum-driven music in various styles. With P16 however, it is as though an unknown force infliltrated the album and attempted deleting half of the music. Traveling through the data center you find yourself walking through broken server rooms in between the clearings revealing the gray sky.


P()~()..48

P48 is my personal favorite. It sets the scene with an icy, shimmering ambient track. Tracks 2, and 3 are the sort of ominous breakcore you'd expect from XoArK, but this is just setting you up to have your expectations broken. ojhugyfghklj; 1 and 2 take you straight into the mainframe. At the core you find SC-230609_002319; a ruthless bombardment of hypnotic repeating patterns. Particles flying everywhere. You almost get decimated in the process if it wasnt for the track slowly decaying and collapsing in on itself, leaving you to crawl out of the dust and regain your footing.
